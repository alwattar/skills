# Threat Model

Trigger when new trust boundaries, external integrations, privileged roles, money movement, upload pipelines or data-sharing flows are introduced. Enumerate assets, components, entry points and untrusted actors. Draw a short data-flow diagram; apply STRIDE or equivalent per boundary. Rank threats by likelihood/impact with explicit assumptions; propose preventative and detective controls, owners and test cases. Include abuse cases, audit, revocation, and incident response. Mark decisions requiring security/product owner approval. No blanket "secure" claims.

## Review output
List evidence, severity, affected boundary, verification and explicit open decisions. Obey project authorization and registered workflows.
