# Campaign Planning

Plan and implement campaigns across search, paid social, organic, referral and offline. Define campaign objective, audience, offer, landing page, channel, UTM taxonomy, cost budget, creative variants, tracking plan, conversion events, attribution and experimentation. Keep LandingPage separate from Campaign and Channel. Use source-specific tracked links and campaign IDs; avoid leaking PII in URLs. For paid integrations prefer dry run or sandbox; do not spend money or connect ad accounts without authorization. Define dashboard, QA checklists, stop conditions and postmortem.

## Deliverable
Include the intended outcome, prerequisites, concrete artifacts, measurable verification and open approvals; follow registered workflows.
