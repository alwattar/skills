# Crm Lifecycle

Design lead management and lifecycle journeys. Define acquisition, qualification, consent/preferences, owner assignment, SLA, status transitions, deduplication, follow-up, quote opportunities and revenue attribution. Separate transactional from marketing communications and respect unsubscribe/suppression and jurisdictional rules. Avoid sending customer data to AI/marketing providers without authorization. Instrument time-to-first-contact, MQL->SQL, win rate and reactivation with provenance. Output CRM schema, templates as drafts, edge cases and acceptance tests.

## Deliverable
Include the intended outcome, prerequisites, concrete artifacts, measurable verification and open approvals; follow registered workflows.
