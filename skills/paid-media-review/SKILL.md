# Paid Media Review

Review planned ad campaigns for compliance with platform rules and product truth. Assess targeting, budget caps, conversion attribution, ad copy, creative permissions, landing-page alignment, account permissions and brand safety. Require explicit owner authorization before spending, pausing live campaigns or changing advertising settings. Check whether pixels and conversion APIs are supported and consent-authorized; do not promise platform approval. Deliver a preflight checklist and channel-specific measurement plan.

## Deliverable
Include the intended outcome, prerequisites, concrete artifacts, measurable verification and open approvals; follow registered workflows.
