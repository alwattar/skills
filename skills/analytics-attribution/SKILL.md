# Analytics Attribution

Design reliable lead-to-revenue analytics. First define event vocabulary and stable campaign/channel/landing-page IDs. Document `page_view -> form_submit -> lead_qualified -> quote -> order -> paid -> retained` transitions as applicable. Distinguish first touch, last touch and multi-touch; conversions may occur offline or weeks later. Deduplicate browser/server events and payments; reject raw PII in UTM or ad identifiers. Produce schema, source-of-truth map, consent rules, identity stitching limits, latency/freshness indicators, dashboard acceptance criteria and test vectors. Do not claim attribution is causal proof or complete cross-device tracking.

## Deliverable
Include the intended outcome, prerequisites, concrete artifacts, measurable verification and open approvals; follow registered workflows.
