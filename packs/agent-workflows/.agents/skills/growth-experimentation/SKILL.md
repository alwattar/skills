# Growth Experimentation

Design ethical experiments for acquisition/conversion/retention. Define primary metric, guardrails, eligible population, variant assignment, sample-size/power approach when possible, observation period, exposure logging, and statistical limitations before launch. Prevent peeking bias and overlapping experiment contamination; use feature flags and reversible rollout. Separate uplift from seasonal or channel mix effects. Protect user privacy, accessibility and SEO. Produce experiment spec, instrumentation QA, results, confidence and decision record. Never invent significance.

## Deliverable
Include the intended outcome, prerequisites, concrete artifacts, measurable verification and open approvals; follow registered workflows.
