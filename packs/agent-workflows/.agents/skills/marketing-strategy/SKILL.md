# Marketing Strategy

Design evidence-based go-to-market strategy. Inputs: product positioning, segments, markets, budget, existing analytics, constraints and approved business goals. Separate awareness, qualified leads, pipeline and revenue; define hypotheses, channel selection, ICP, value proposition, funnel, budget allocation and KPI targets with assumptions. Cross-check claims against verified product capabilities. Require approval for spending and public claims. Never invent market statistics, ROI, testimonials or customer results. Route tracking/PII to privacy-review and analytics-attribution.

## Deliverable
Include the intended outcome, prerequisites, concrete artifacts, measurable verification and open approvals; follow registered workflows.
