# Content Marketing

Develop factual, audience-specific content briefs, messaging and editorial calendars. Specify target persona, search intent, proof points, approved offer, funnel stage, CTA, source requirements and editorial review. Maintain differentiated localization rather than machine-translation claims. Disclose advertising/sponsorship where required; avoid fake factories, certificates, partnerships, testimonials, social proof or fabricated statistics. Produce reusable content blocks and measurement hypotheses, with owner sign-off for commercial/legal claims.

## Deliverable
Include the intended outcome, prerequisites, concrete artifacts, measurable verification and open approvals; follow registered workflows.
