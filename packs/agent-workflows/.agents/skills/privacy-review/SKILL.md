# Privacy Review

Use on user tracking, identifiers, consent, marketing integrations, exports, retention, personal data or profiling. Map controller/processor roles, data categories, lawful basis, purposes, processors, destinations, data flows, retention, access and erasure requests. Identify EU ePrivacy/GDPR consent gates when applicable; do not infer permission from a general privacy-policy link. Separate necessary measurement from advertising trackers. Data minimization and privacy-by-default; PII must not leak to logs, pixels or URLs. Deliver a data-flow inventory, compliance open questions for counsel, consent test cases, and deployment blockers. Do not state legal compliance as guaranteed.

## Review output
List evidence, severity, affected boundary, verification and explicit open decisions. Obey project authorization and registered workflows.
